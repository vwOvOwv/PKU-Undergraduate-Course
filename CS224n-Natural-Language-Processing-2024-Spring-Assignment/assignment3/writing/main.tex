    \item The convolution layer after embedding combines individual token embeddings, 
    which gives model better semantic representations of phrases consisting of multiple tokens.
    \item \begin{enumerate}[label=\roman*., left=0pt, labelsep=1em, itemsep=1em]
    \item The NMT model translates the phrase 贼人 into the singular form \textit{the 
    culprit was} while the gold reference is the plural form \textit{the culprits were}. 
    The error occurs due to morphological ambiguity in the source language (Chinese), 
    where the noun 贼人 does not explicitly indicate grammatical number (singular vs. plural). 
    The model likely defaults to the singular form based on its frequency in the training data, 
    as it lacks sufficient contextual clues or explicit number markers to infer the plural sense.
    To fix such errors, nouns in the source corpus could be pre-processed with explicit entity 
    number tags.
    \item The model repeats \textit{resources have been exhausted} and omits 
    \textit{no space to accommodate these people}. This error may originate from 
    attention mechanisms. The model allocate too much attention on the second part
    资源已经用尽. A possible fix is to change the attention mechanism during decoding.
    \item The model translates 今天是国殇日 into \textit{today's day}, seeming to take
    the source as a clause, and 国殇 is omitted in translation. In training data, 国殇
    appears only once, thus it's hard for the model to learn a robust representation for
    this word. To fix, more sentences including 国殇 should be added into the corpus.
    \item The model ignores the meaning of 唔做 during translation. It seems that the
    model doesn't understand the causal relationship between 唔做 and 唔错. The main reason for
    this may be lack of Cantonese showing such causal relationship in the training corpus. A 
    possible fix for this can be adding more Cantonese texts with more complicated logics into
    the training data.
    \end{enumerate}
